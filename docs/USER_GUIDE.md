# Smart Farmer Assistant — User Guide

Welcome to the **Smart Farmer Assistant** platform! This user guide walks you through using the application for optimal farm management.

## 1. Registering & Logging In
- Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:5000/auth/register`.
- Select your role (`Farmer`, `Agricultural Advisor`, or `System Administrator`).
- Enter username, email, password, and optional regional details.

## 2. Managing Farms & Fields
- Click **Farms & Fields** on the sidebar menu.
- Click **Add New Farm** to register property details (location, acreage, soil type).
- Click **View & Add Fields** to divide your farm into manageable field plots.

## 3. Running Soil Health Checks
- Go to **Soil Analysis**.
- Select a field plot and input soil N, P, K, pH, Moisture %, and Organic Carbon %.
- Click **Run Soil Health Analysis** to view your Soil Health Score (0-100) and improvement plan.

## 4. ML Crop Recommendation
- Go to **Crop Recommendation**.
- Input soil NPK, temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall.
- View top 3 recommended crops with suitability match %, duration, and yield expectations.

## 5. Leaf Disease Diagnosis
- Go to **Disease Diagnosis**.
- Upload a leaf photograph from your camera or gallery.
- Receive instant offline diagnosis, confidence rating, organic treatments, and chemical guidance.

## 6. Financial Management & Reports
- Use **Expenses Log** and **Harvest Revenue** to log financial transactions.
- Click **Profit Forecast** to calculate break-even prices and net margins.
- Click **Export PDF Report** on your dashboard to download a standalone PDF summary.
